The Challenge

As part of a school project, our team of six worked with the existing Underbara Unge app. The task was to analyse the existing service and explore how its user experience could be improved. We focused on identifying pain points from our previous research and user journey, then used these insights to create a high-fidelity prototype with a focus on usability and accessibility. The goal was to make the experience clearer and easier to use while following WCAG 2.1 guidelines and considering the needs of the people using the service.

The Goals

•Improve the overall usability of the app
•Make information easier to find and understand
•Create a clear and consistent user experience
•Improve accessibility by applying WCAG 2.1 guidelines
•Design a clickable high-fidelity prototype that supports a complete user flow

Research

We started by looking at the existing Underbara Unge app and the research we had already conducted during the course. Our research included questionnaires, stakeholder interviews and work with personas and customer journeys. We also had the opportunity to communicate directly with the creator of Underbara Unge, which gave us a better understanding of the app, its purpose and the people it was created for. By combining the research with the existing app, we could identify areas where the experience could be clearer, easier to navigate and more accessible.

Structure

Using the research and identified pain points as a starting point, we began exploring how the app could be structured to create a clearer and more intuitive experience. We looked at how information, navigation and different parts of the user journey could work together before moving into the visual design.

Wireframes & Prototyping

Once we had identified the areas we wanted to improve, we started exploring different ways to solve them. We began with wireframes to focus on the structure, content and user flow before developing the visual design. Working together allowed us to explore different ideas and refine the experience as a team. I contributed to the wireframes and prototyping in Figma, helping develop the screens from early concepts into a connected high-fidelity prototype.

Visual Design

Once the structure and user flow were in place, we developed the visual direction for the app. We focused on creating a clear and consistent interface that felt welcoming while still being easy to navigate. Colour, typography, spacing and reusable UI elements were considered together to create a more cohesive experience. Accessibility was also an important part of the design. We used WCAG 2.1 as a guide when working with colour contrast, typography, navigation and interactive elements. I contributed to the visual design and helped develop the high-fidelity screens in Figma.
