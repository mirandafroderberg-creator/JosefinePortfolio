The Challenge

During my internship at Byteatern Kalmar Länsteater, I was given the task of creating promotional material to promote the membership and attract new members.
One of the main challenges was that many people did not know that Byteatern Kalmar Länsteater offered a membership at all. The communication therefore needed to make the membership easy to notice and understand, while still feeling recognisable as Byteatern Kalmar Länsteater.
I created a small promotional campaign consisting of a poster, flyer and animation, designed to communicate the same message across both print and digital formats.

The Goals

• Make the membership more visible and easier to notice
• Clearly communicate what the membership offers
• Create promotional material that attracts new members
• Keep the communication recognisable as Byteatern Kalmar Länsteater
• Create a cohesive campaign across print and digital formats

Visual Design & Final Campaign

I worked with Byteatern Kalmar Länsteater's existing visual identity to create a promotional campaign that felt recognisable while making the membership message more noticeable.

I used bold typography, colour and simple graphic elements to create a clear and engaging visual direction. The same visual language was adapted across the different formats, creating a cohesive campaign while allowing each piece to work in its own context.

Final campaign

Animation

The animation was created for both social media and the screens in Byteatern Kalmar Länsteater's lobby. I used movement, timing and transitions to make the message engaging and easy to understand at a glance.
Together, the poster and animation created a cohesive campaign across both physical and digital spaces.
